# Changelog

All notable ClinDrift research-prototype changes are recorded here.

## v0.1.0-alpha - 2026-10-25

### Added

- Initial public alpha research release.
- Medication dosage-drift detection.
- Allergy contradiction detection.
- Duration-drift detection.
- Preservation checks for supported measurements.
- Evidence-linked findings and transparent integrity scoring.
- Human-review signalling.
- Controlled Clinical Drift Mutation Lab.
- Downloadable JSON audit reports.
- Streamlit research interface.
- Automated tests and CI.
- Docker/deployment support.
- Research methodology, evaluation, threat-model, limitations, and demo documentation.
- Contributor guidance for safe synthetic/de-identified research contributions.

### Validation boundary

- The bundled controlled mutation example contains three deliberately introduced changes and currently detects all three.
- This is a controlled software/research result, not a claim of clinical accuracy, sensitivity, specificity, safety, or generalisable performance.
- ClinDrift is not a medical device and is not intended for diagnosis, treatment, certification, regulatory determination, or clinical decision-making.
