# 60-second demo video script

Use only synthetic or appropriately de-identified text.

## 0-8 seconds — problem

Show the ClinDrift landing page.

Narration:

> "AI can transform clinical text while quietly changing an important fact. ClinDrift asks one question: did clinically important meaning survive?"

## 8-25 seconds — controlled mutation

Open the Clinical Drift Mutation Lab and introduce a dosage, allergy, or duration change.

Narration:

> "The built-in mutation lab creates a controlled change so the integrity-checking workflow can be tested reproducibly."

## 25-42 seconds — inspect the finding

Run the integrity check and show the finding.

Narration:

> "ClinDrift links the source value, transformed value, supporting text, severity, and rationale so the flag can be inspected rather than blindly trusted."

## 42-52 seconds — human review

Show the integrity score and human-review status.

Narration:

> "The score is a prototype assurance signal, not a clinical safety certificate. Safety-relevant drift still requires human review."

## 52-60 seconds — call to action

Narration:

> "ClinDrift is an open research prototype. Star it to follow the research, fork it to test new synthetic drift cases, or contribute reproducible improvements."

## Recording notes

- Never show identifiable patient information.
- Do not claim clinical accuracy from the three-mutation controlled example.
- Keep the alpha/research-prototype status visible.
